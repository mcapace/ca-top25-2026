      The Top 25 process

      How a cigar makes the list

      1. Step 1

        We review the year's highest-scoring cigars and assemble a new tasting of only those.

      2. Step 2

        We buy them again, re-band them and smoke them blind.

      3. Step 3

        The top scorers are smoked against each other over multiple rounds.

      4. Step 4

        A new list of 25 cigars is assembled.
